TITLE:
Physics-Informed Equivariant Graph Neural Networks for De Novo 3D Molecular 
Generation in Protein Binding Pockets

ABSTRACT:
De novo drug design requires generating 3D molecular structures that fit spatially 
and thermodynamically within specific receptor binding sites. Traditional deep learning 
generative models often output geometrically invalid or clash-heavy poses. Here, we present 
a Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) framework integrating E(3)-Equivariant Graph 
Neural Networks (EGNN) with a Differentiable Physical Force Field engine. By embedding 
Soft-Core Lennard-Jones potentials and distance-dependent electrostatic interactions 
directly into the loss gradient, our model ensures spatial equivariance while enforcing 
physical non-overlapping constraints. Experimental evaluation demonstrates superior pose 
stability, high QED drug-likeness (>0.58), and full Lipinski compliance without requiring 
post-generation docking relaxation.

METHODOLOGY HIGHLIGHTS:
1. Model Architecture: 
   - Node Embedding Layer mapping 19 atomic features (element one-hot, hybridization, formal charge).
   - Custom E(3)-Equivariant Graph Convolutional Layers updating spatial coordinates (x_i) 
     and node features (h_i) iteratively.
   
2. Differentiable Physics Loss Engine:
   - Soft-Core Lennard-Jones Potential: Avoids gradient explosion at short interatomic distances.
   - Distance-Dependent Electrostatics: Incorporates implicit solvation via dielectric constant epsilon(r) = 4r.
   - Total Loss Function: L_total = L_coord (MSE) + lambda * L_physics.

3. Results Summary:
   - Rapid convergence within 20 epochs using AdamW optimizer (lr = 1e-3).
   - Generated molecules exhibit optimal Lipinski Rule of 5 properties and realistic binding poses.
